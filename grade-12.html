TOK Year 2 (Grade 12) · Lesson plan Anchor: "Knowledge and technology" · Knowledge Framework: Perspectives · TOK Concept: Objectivity
Unit / Anchor text
Knowledge and technology: Where does AI bias come from?
Objective SWBAT…
Scholars will be able to analyze how bias enters an AI system at three stages, its data, its design, and its deployment, and identify where bias entered in a given AI output.
Why It Matters
Year 2 writes the Essay and leans on sources. If AI can carry bias, a knower has to see where that bias enters before trusting an AI-produced claim as evidence.
IB Framework
Knowledge Framework: Perspectives (how background shapes the knowledge produced)
TOK Concept: Objectivity
Conceptual / Knowledge Question: To what extent can knowledge produced by AI be objective?
Learner Profile: Thinkers · Reflective
ATLs: Thinking (critical) · Research
Key Points
  • Bias can enter at three stages: the training data, the system's design, and how it is deployed.
  • "The data does not lie" is a myth; data carries the past.
  • Spotting the stage is the first step to judging reliability.
Central Vocabulary / Command Term
Command terms: analyze · identify · justify. AI AI terms: training data · algorithmic bias · deployment · objectivity.
Potential Misunderstandings
  • "A computer is neutral." Bias can enter at several stages.
  • "Bias is only in the data." Design and deployment introduce it too.
Do First · Do Now 7 min
ROUTINE The Year 2 argument-building opener. AI Scholars read a short AI output and note anything that looks slanted or unsupported.
Introduction to New Material & Teacher Model 15 min · I do
AI Scenario clip of a real biased-AI case. The teacher teaches the three stages where bias enters, data, design, deployment, with an example of each, then models tracing one biased output back to the stage it came from.
Guided Practice 13 min · we do
ROUTINE The prescribed-title "speed dating" discussion. AI In rotating pairs, scholars map where bias entered two sample AI outputs, naming the stage for each.
Independent Practice 5 min · you do
AI Each scholar analyzes one new AI output alone and marks which stage, data, design, or deployment, the bias entered, with a reason.
Formative Assessment / Exit Ticket 5 min
Given one AI output, identify where bias entered (data, design, or deployment) and justify the choice in one or two sentences. Exemplar in the Teacher Guide.
Feeds the IB-graded pieces IB
Essay: sharpens source evaluation for prescribed-title research. Concept: builds the objectivity-versus-bias line the Essay can argue. Supports the Essay; never generates it. Note: the academic-integrity and cited-use content is now its own lesson in the Ethics part.
How this becomes finished lessons. For each lesson we first build the 18 elements the grant requires (videos, in-class activity, teacher guide, assessment, and so on) with subject-matter input, then deliver the lesson to you in this exact format for your approval. What each of the 18 elements IS, what the expert provides, and what the design team builds. This is for everyone, the partner and the expert alike. The elements are the same for every grade.

The audience everything is written for: IB Diploma Theory of Knowledge seniors (grade 12), about 17 to 18 years old, senior fall only, mixed reading levels with emergent bilingual scholars, Chromebooks; the course carries 1 TOK credit. Reading-level rule: scholar-facing prose at grade 7 to 9 readability; every video captioned; academic vocabulary taught with cognates for emergent bilingual scholars.

Foundation

1 · Learning outcome one observable sentence

What a scholar can do after the lesson, in observable verbs. The expert names the capability; the design team phrases it and aligns the assessment.

2 · Key concepts the 3 to 5 ideas that must survive

Each maps to an assessment item and usually a video. The expert provides the concepts and the misconceptions that fight them. In TOK these become a knowledge question.

3 · Vocabulary, Beck's three tiers EB instruction standard

Tier 1 everyday · Tier 2 academic words (with cognates) · Tier 3 domain terms. Focus on Tiers 2 and 3; AI terms sit beside the IB command terms.

4 · Standards mapping the course's governing document

The standard the lesson satisfies. TOK is a Texas-approved innovative course with no TEKS, so alignment is to the IB Theory of Knowledge Guide, which defines the course's expectations, not a TEKS code. Design-team task.

Scholar materials

5 · Student-facing content

Hook → concept (with video) → guided try → independent try → close. The expert supplies substance and a worked example; AI drafts to the audience; the expert signs off.

6 · Concept videos ×2

One short captioned video per key-concept cluster: one idea, one analogy. The videos carry the teaching.

7 · Scenario video

The idea working or failing in a real moment. In TOK it also seeds a knowledge question.

8 · In-class activity

Hands-on practice: stump it · catch it · fix it · judge it. The expert says what practice and what failure look like.

9 · Going-deeper segment

The knowledge-question debate that feeds the Essay and Exhibition. The expert provides the one insight they wish scholars knew.

10 · Extension activity

A deeper optional task for scholars who want more.

Teacher enablement

11 · Teacher Guide

Makes the lesson teachable by a non-expert. The expert provides the misconceptions, the why, and the pitfalls.

12 · Teacher video

The guide's elevator version, watched once before teaching.

Assessment, supports & future

13 · Formative assessment

Built to auto-grade where possible; every item maps to a key concept. The expert provides one assessment idea and the misconceptions that become distractors.

14 · Answer key / rubric

Scoring and which misses signal which reteach. Lives in the Teacher Guide.

15 · Pre-assessment / baseline

Short baseline before the unit; mirrors the formative banks. Runs in the partner's systems; the university receives de-identified aggregates.

16 · Discussion scaffolds

Teacher talking points + scholar sentence stems, bilingual-friendly, using Tier 2 vocabulary.

17 · AI-tutor knowledge base

Content distilled into retrievable chunks so the future tutor answers grounded in approved content. Internal until the tutor ships.

18 · Language Support Pack EB support, bundled

One reviewable deliverable bundling the lesson’s language scaffolds for emergent-bilingual scholars: a tiered glossary (Tiers 2 and 3 taught, concept-critical Tier 1 glossed for newcomers, with cognates), sentence frames and stems for every activity and discussion, and bilingual response options for open items. Built from the vocabulary, discussion, and assessment elements; lands in the TOK Accommodations / Modifications menu.
